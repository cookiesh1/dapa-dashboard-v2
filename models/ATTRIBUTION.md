# Map data

World Imagery: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, GIS User Community and tile-specific contributors (shown in the map's credits). https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer

Terrain3D: Esri / ArcGIS WorldElevation3D. https://elevation3d.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation3D/Terrain3D/ImageServer

Both services are streamed online. No offline imagery redistribution is included. Provider credits remain visible.
